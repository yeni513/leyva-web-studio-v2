# Google Ads Starter — Version Dios

Help set up a safe starter Google Ads service for a local business.

## Important rule

Management fee is separate from ad spend. The client pays Google separately.

## Do not promise

Never guarantee clicks, leads, sales, calls, rankings, or revenue.

## Setup steps

1. Confirm business goal: calls, WhatsApp, forms, visits, orders.
2. Confirm service area/radius.
3. Confirm landing page quality.
4. Define budget recommendation.
5. Build keyword list.
6. Build negative keyword list.
7. Draft ad copy.
8. Define conversion actions.
9. Launch Search campaign first when intent is strong.
10. Review weekly.
11. Send monthly report.

## Output

Create campaign plan, keywords, negative keywords, ad examples, budget recommendation, tracking checklist, and report template.
