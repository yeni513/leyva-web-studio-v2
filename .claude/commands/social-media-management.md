# Social Media Management — Version Dios

Create monthly social media management system.

## Packages

- Social Basic: 8 posts/month
- Social Pro: 12 posts/month
- Social Premium: 16 posts/month + stories

## Monthly workflow

1. Collect photos/videos/offers.
2. Create content calendar.
3. Write captions.
4. Design posts or edit reels.
5. Send for approval.
6. Schedule posts.
7. Review metrics.
8. Send report.

## Output

Create content calendar, post ideas, captions, approval workflow, and monthly report template.
