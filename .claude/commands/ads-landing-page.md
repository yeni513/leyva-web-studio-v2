# Ads Landing Page — Version Dios

Create landing page for Google Ads or Meta Ads traffic.

## Goal

Convert paid traffic into calls, WhatsApp, bookings, quote requests, orders, or leads.

## Rules

- One clear offer
- One primary CTA
- Fast loading
- Mobile-first
- Minimal distractions
- Trust proof near top
- Short form/direct contact
- Local relevance
- FAQ

## Sections

Hero, trust proof, benefits, services/products, social proof, contact/lead form, FAQ, final CTA.

## Output

Build focused landing page ready for paid traffic.
